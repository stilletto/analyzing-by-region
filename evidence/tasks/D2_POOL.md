[reserve-stock-toctou] (slices: 1,2,9) MECHANISM: `reserve_stock` reads `available` under `_inv_lock` (36-37), releases the lock, compares outside it (39), then re-acquires to decrement (43-44), so two threads can both see the last units and both subtract. CONSEQUENCE: stock is promised that does not exist, clustered on hot SKUs during promotions and sellouts, and discovered days later by a picker who cannot find the goods.

CONSEQUENCE: the SKU silently stops selling for all customers until a restart reseeds inventory or enough cancellations lift it above zero, and it is reported as a merchandising or catalogue bug rather than a concurrency one.
